About Talk Aurelia

Personal Karbi Language Learning Aid

This application has been developed for personal learning and educational purposes, particularly to facilitate learning the Karbi language at one's own convenience and pace.

It is not intended for commercial use, promotion or any purpose that may harm, offend, disrespect or discriminate against any individual, community or group.

⚠️ Accuracy Disclaimer

Every effort has been made to ensure the accuracy and reliability of the information, translations, meanings and pronunciations provided in this application. However, some information may be incomplete, inaccurate or misleading. Variations in local dialects, expressions, meanings and pronunciation may also exist.

Users are advised to verify important information with native Karbi speakers, language experts or other reliable sources before relying on it.

This application is intended solely as a learning aid and should not be considered an authoritative or definitive source of the Karbi language.
